UNDERSTANDING DIGITAL SAFETY

What Is Digital Safety?

Digital safety involves protecting yourself, your information, your devices and your online activities from digital risks while using technology.

📱

Device Protection

Keep your devices updated and use trusted security features to reduce digital risks.

🌐

Safe Internet Use

Browse responsibly, verify websites and avoid suspicious online content.

🧠

Digital Awareness

Think critically about online messages, information, offers and requests.

KNOW THE RISKS

Common Digital Threats

Understanding common threats can help you recognize warning signs and take safer actions.

🎣

Phishing

Phishing involves deceptive messages or websites that attempt to trick people into sharing information or taking unsafe actions.

Warning sign: Unexpected messages asking for passwords, OTPs or urgent account actions.
🦠

Malware

Malware is harmful software that can disrupt devices, damage files or compromise information.

Warning sign: Unexpected downloads, suspicious software or unusual device behaviour.
🎭

Online Impersonation

Someone may pretend to be another person, organization or trusted account online.

Warning sign: An account makes unusual requests or asks you to trust it without verification.
💸

Online Scams

Scams use misleading offers, messages or promises to trick people into losing money or sharing sensitive information.

Warning sign: Unrealistic rewards, unexpected payment requests or pressure to act quickly.
🔓

Account Compromise

An account may be accessed by someone without the account owner's permission.

Warning sign: Unknown login alerts, changed settings or unfamiliar account activity.
📡

Unsafe Public Wi-Fi

Public networks may not always provide reliable protection for your online activity.

Warning sign: Unknown networks, unclear network names or requests to install unfamiliar software.
MESSAGE AWARENESS

How to Recognize Suspicious Messages

Some messages try to create fear, excitement or urgency so that people act without thinking.

📧
Example of a suspicious message

Educational example only

Subject: Urgent! Your account needs attention!

Your account will supposedly be suspended. Click an unfamiliar link immediately and provide your information to avoid losing access.

🚩 Warning signs

  • Creates unnecessary urgency.
  • Requests personal or account information.
  • Contains an unfamiliar link.
  • Uses threatening or alarming language.
Safer action: Do not interact with suspicious links. Verify the message using the official website, app or contact details of the organization.
SMART BROWSING

Practices for Safer Browsing

Make your browsing experience safer by following these simple practices.

🔍

Verify Websites

Check website names carefully and use official websites when accessing services.

⬇️

Download Responsibly

Download files and applications only from trustworthy sources.

🔄

Update Your Devices

Install updates for your operating system, browser and applications.

🔑

Secure Your Accounts

Use unique passwords and additional security features where available.

🚫

Avoid Suspicious Links

Do not click unexpected links or open unfamiliar attachments.

🧑‍🏫

Ask for Help

If something seems suspicious, ask a trusted adult or knowledgeable person before acting.

EVERYDAY SITUATIONS

What Would You Do?

Understand how to respond to common digital safety situations.

SITUATION 01

You Receive an Unexpected Prize

A message claims you have won a prize but asks for payment or personal details before you can receive it.

Safe response: Treat it as suspicious. Do not pay or share information. Verify the offer independently.
SITUATION 02

A File Is Sent by an Unknown Person

An unfamiliar account sends you a file and asks you to download it.

Safe response: Do not open it just because someone sent it. Verify the sender and use trusted security practices.
SITUATION 03

Your Account Shows Unknown Activity

You notice unfamiliar activity or receive an unexpected login notification.

Safe response: Secure your account using its official recovery and security settings. Inform a trusted adult if needed.
SITUATION 04

A Website Makes Unrealistic Promises

A website promises extraordinary rewards or benefits for very little effort.

Safe response: Do not trust unrealistic claims. Verify information and avoid sharing money or sensitive details.
