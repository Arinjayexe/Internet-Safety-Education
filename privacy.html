PRIVACY BASICS

What Is Online Privacy?

Online privacy refers to how your personal information, activities and digital identity are protected and controlled while using the internet.

👤

Personal Information

Information that can identify you or reveal details about your life.

  • Full name
  • Phone number
  • Home address
  • School information
📱

Digital Footprint

The traces of information you leave behind when using the internet.

  • Posts and comments
  • Search activity
  • Uploaded pictures
  • Online interactions
🔑

Account Security

Protecting your online accounts from unauthorized access.

  • Strong passwords
  • Two-factor authentication
  • Account recovery settings
  • Secure login habits
WHY IT MATTERS

Why Should You Protect Your Privacy?

🛡️

Prevent Identity Misuse

Protect personal details that could be misused by others.

🔒

Protect Your Accounts

Reduce the risk of unauthorized access to your online accounts.

📷

Control What You Share

Decide what information, photos and activities you want to share publicly.

🧠

Make Informed Decisions

Understand privacy policies, permissions and the information apps request.

PRACTICAL TIPS

Ways to Protect Your Privacy

Small online habits can make a big difference in protecting your information.

01

Use Strong Passwords

Use long, unique passwords for different accounts and avoid easily guessed information.

02

Review Privacy Settings

Check who can see your profile, posts, pictures and personal information.

03

Limit Data Sharing

Avoid sharing sensitive information unless it is genuinely necessary.

04

Check App Permissions

Review whether apps really need access to your location, camera or contacts.

05

Think Before Posting

Consider whether a post could reveal private details or cause problems later.

06

Enable Extra Protection

Use two-factor authentication when available to add another layer of account security.

⚠️
BE CAREFUL

Think Carefully Before Sharing

Never casually share sensitive information with strangers, unknown websites or unverified online contacts.

  • Passwords and OTPs
  • Banking and payment details
  • Private documents
  • Live location with unknown people
  • Private photographs or personal information
SOCIAL MEDIA PRIVACY

Manage Your Digital Identity

Social media allows us to connect with others, but our profiles may reveal more information than we intend. Understanding privacy controls helps us manage what we share.

👥

Control Your Audience

Review who can view your posts, stories, profile information and activity. Avoid assuming that every online audience is trustworthy.

📍

Be Careful With Location

Avoid publicly sharing your live location, daily routines, travel plans or details that reveal where you regularly spend time.

📸

Review Before Uploading

Photos may reveal backgrounds, documents, school details or other information that you did not intend to share.

🧑‍🤝‍🧑

Choose Connections Carefully

Do not accept every friend or follower request. Unknown accounts may not be who they claim to be.

WEBSITE TRACKING

Understanding Cookies

Cookies are small pieces of information stored by websites in your browser. They can help websites remember settings and understand activity.

🍪

What Are Cookies?

Cookies may remember your login session, language preferences, shopping cart or other website settings.

📊

Why Are They Used?

Some cookies support website functionality, while others may help measure website usage or personalize advertisements.

⚙️

Manage Cookie Preferences

Review cookie notices and browser settings. Where available, choose whether optional tracking cookies are allowed.

Privacy reminder: Essential cookies may be needed for a website to work. Optional cookies may serve other purposes. Read the cookie notice instead of automatically accepting every option.
REAL-LIFE EXAMPLES

Privacy Decisions in Everyday Life

Everyday online activities can involve privacy risks. Consider how you would respond to these situations.

SCENARIO 01

A Stranger Requests Your Details

Someone online asks for your phone number, school name and personal photographs.

Safer response: Do not share the information. Block or report the account if necessary and tell a trusted adult.
SCENARIO 02

An App Requests Many Permissions

A simple app requests access to your contacts, microphone, location and photographs.

Safer response: Review whether these permissions are necessary. Deny unnecessary access and consider using another app if appropriate.
SCENARIO 03

You Want to Post a Photograph

Your photograph contains personal documents or location details in the background.

Safer response: Check the photograph carefully and remove or hide sensitive information before sharing.
SCENARIO 04

You Receive a Privacy Alert

You receive a message claiming that your account will be closed unless you immediately click a link.

Safer response: Do not click the message link. Check your account using the official website or app.
