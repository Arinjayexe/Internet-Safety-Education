Your Guide to a
Safer Internet

Simple everyday habits can help protect your accounts, personal information, devices and online experiences. Follow these guidelines to use the internet responsibly.

10 Golden Rules of Internet Safety

Follow these essential rules whenever you use the internet.

01

Protect Personal Information

Never publicly share your address, phone number, passwords or private details.

02

Use Strong Passwords

Create long, unique passwords for your accounts and never share them.

03

Think Before Clicking

Avoid suspicious links, unexpected attachments and unknown websites.

04

Verify Information

Check information through reliable sources before believing or sharing it.

05

Respect Others

Be polite, respectful and responsible in online conversations.

06

Keep Devices Updated

Install updates to improve security and fix software vulnerabilities.

07

Check Privacy Settings

Review account privacy settings and control what others can see.

08

Download Carefully

Download applications and files only from trusted sources.

09

Report Suspicious Activity

Report scams, abusive behaviour and suspicious accounts.

10

Ask for Help

Speak to a trusted adult if something online makes you uncomfortable.

Password Security

Passwords are one of the most important ways to protect your digital accounts.

What Makes a Password Strong?

  • Use long passwords or passphrases.
  • Use different passwords for different accounts.
  • Avoid names, birthdays and predictable patterns.
  • Use a trusted password manager if appropriate.
  • Enable two-factor authentication.

What is Two-Factor Authentication?

Two-factor authentication (2FA) adds an extra security step when logging into an account. In addition to a password, you may need another verification method.

This extra layer helps protect an account even if someone learns your password.

Safe Downloads & Device Protection

📥

Trusted Downloads

Download software from official websites or trusted app stores. Avoid unknown download links.

🔄

Regular Updates

Keep your operating system, browser and applications updated.

🔒

Screen Lock

Use a screen lock on your phone, laptop and other personal devices.

🛡️

Security Protection

Use built-in security features and trusted protection tools available on your device.

💾

Back Up Important Data

Keep backups of important documents and files in a secure location.

🚪

Log Out

Sign out of accounts when using shared computers or public devices.

Social Media Safety

Social media connects people, but responsible use is important for protecting your privacy and safety.

Before Posting

Think about whether the content reveals personal information or could affect you or someone else.

Accepting Requests

Be cautious with friend requests and followers from people you do not know.

Location Sharing

Avoid publicly sharing your real-time location or travel plans.

Account Privacy

Review who can see your posts, profile information and activity.

Safe Online Communication

  • Be respectful while chatting or commenting.
  • Do not share private conversations without permission.
  • Do not trust strangers with sensitive information.
  • Never feel pressured to send private information or images.
  • Be careful when someone online asks for money or personal details.
  • Report threatening or suspicious conversations.
